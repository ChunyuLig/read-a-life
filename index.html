范仲淹Fan Zhongyan

FAN ZHONGYAN · 文正 · 989–1052范仲淹 · 989–1052
⟡ 一句话本质⟡ IN ONE LINE

一个两岁丧父、划粥断齑苦读出身的寒门子,以"先天下之忧而忧,后天下之乐而乐"立身——文能写《岳阳楼记》,武能御西夏,政能主新政,是中国士大夫的人格标杆。A fatherless boy who studied on frozen porridge, and lived by his own immortal line—"be first to worry about the world, last to enjoy it." Scholar, frontier general, and reformer—the moral standard of the Chinese scholar-official.

PART 1

他是谁Who He Is

989 年生,两岁丧父,母亲改嫁,他随继父姓,寄人篱下。少年在寺庙苦读,穷到每天煮一锅粥,凝固后划成四块、就着腌菜吃("划粥断齑")。1015 年考中进士,恢复范姓。此后他一身兼三任:为官清廉干练(筑海堤),屡屡直言进谏被贬而不悔;西夏来犯时出镇陕西,"军中有一范,西贼闻之惊破胆";又主持"庆历新政"改革吏治。一篇《岳阳楼记》写尽他的胸怀:"先天下之忧而忧,后天下之乐而乐"。 Born 989; his father died when he was two. Raised under a stepfather's name, he studied in a temple in such poverty that he boiled one pot of porridge a day, let it set, cut it into four, and ate it with pickles. He passed the imperial exam in 1015 and reclaimed his family name. He then mastered three roles at once: an honest, capable official; an outspoken minister demoted again and again without regret; a frontier commander who held off the Western Xia; and the reformer behind the "Qingli New Policies". His essay on Yueyang Tower holds his whole creed: "Be first in the realm to worry, last to enjoy."

B核心动机和恐惧Core Drives & Fears
▲ 驱动他的▲ What drove him

"以天下为己任"的担当——报国济民、革除弊政、教化育人。他要的不是高位,而是有用于天下。A vow to carry "the whole realm" as his charge—to serve the people, reform a sick government, and educate a generation.

▼ 他害怕的▼ What he feared

怕的是尸位素餐、不能尽责;不怕贬谪、不怕得罪权贵——"宁鸣而死,不默而生"。He feared holding a post and failing his duty—not demotion or powerful enemies. "Better to cry out and die than stay silent and live."

C自己眼中的自己How He Saw Himself

他以"古仁人"自期:"不以物喜,不以己悲;居庙堂之高则忧其民,处江湖之远则忧其君。"无论得志失意,他都把自己看作一个为天下分忧的人——位置会变,这份担当不变。He measured himself against the sages of old: "Not delighted by things, not saddened by self; high at court, he worries for the people; far in the wilds, he worries for the throne." In triumph or disgrace, he saw himself as one who shoulders the world's cares—the post might change; the burden never did.

Steal his sense of duty and his backbone; just add a little more flexibility and patience, so ideals can actually take root.

PART 1

他是谁Who He Is

1037 年生于四川眉山的书香世家(父苏洵、弟苏辙合称"三苏")。21 岁高中进士,文名动京师。一生却因卷入新旧党争而三起三落:1079 年"乌台诗案"几乎丧命,此后接连被贬黄州、惠州、儋州(海南),越贬越远。可越是落魄,他的诗词书画越是登峰造极。他是诗、词、文、书、画样样登顶的全才,也是会种地、做东坡肉、修苏堤、办学校的"百姓之友"。 Born 1037 into a literary family in Sichuan (he, his father and brother are the "Three Su"). A top-ranked scholar at 21, his fame shook the capital. But caught in factional wars, he rose and fell three times: the 1079 "Crow Terrace" poetry case nearly killed him, and he was banished ever farther—to Huangzhou, Huizhou, and Hainan. Yet the lower he fell, the higher his art soared. A supreme master of poetry, prose, calligraphy and painting—and a friend of the common people who farmed, cooked, built dikes and founded schools.
